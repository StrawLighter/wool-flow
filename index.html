Winding the yarn…

How to play

  • Tap a yarn ball in the pile. It hops into a cushion and kittens of that colour start pulling stitches.
  • Knitting unravels from the top. A stitch is loose only when the stitch above it is gone. Look for the little white thread on loose stitches.
  • The number is how many stitches the ball still needs. When it hits zero the cushion frees up.
  • A ball with nothing loose to pull just sits there. Fill every cushion with sleeping kittens and the level is lost.
  • Tangled balls are two-tone: they unravel the first colour, then turn into the second.
  • Deeper balls unlock once the balls resting on them are gone.
  • Boosters: +Cushion adds a cushion, Hook pulls a ball back out of its cushion, Snip splits a tangled ball's second colour back into the pile. Win without boosters for three stars.
