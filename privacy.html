Wool Flow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The short version

Wool Flow does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (which levels you have unlocked, your star ratings and whether sound is muted) on your device only. This data is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network access

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Children

Because we collect no data, the game is safe for players of all ages, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/StrawLighter/wool-flow/issues.
