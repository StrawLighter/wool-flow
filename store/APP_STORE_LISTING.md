## Description
Wool Flow is a cosy puzzle about kittens, yarn and knitting that comes undone.

Every level is a knitted picture made of tiny stitches. Tap a ball of yarn and a team of kittens of that colour trots over, stops, and gives the stitch a gentle pat to pull it loose. The twist: knitting unravels from the top down, so a stitch only comes free once the stitch above it is gone. Plan your taps, keep the cushions from clogging, and watch the picture disappear thread by thread.

FEATURES
- 100 hand-checked levels across 10 chapters, every one proven solvable
- Knitted pictures to unravel: strawberries, owls, frogs, robots, patterns and more
- New twists as you go: wrapped mystery yarn, tied bundles, knitting bags, knotted balls with hidden needles, and zipped patches
- Tangled two-colour yarn that changes colour mid-pull
- Calm, ASMR-paced animation with soft sounds
- Boosters when you are stuck: extra cushion, hook and snip
- Earn up to three stars per level
- Works fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no data collected

Perfect for a few relaxed minutes or a long unwinding evening. Grab your needles. The kittens are ready.

## App Review notes
No login or account is needed. The app is fully offline and has no in-app purchases, ads, tracking or network requests. Tap Play and start at level 1. Levels unlock in order as each one is cleared.
Boosters (+Cushion, Hook, Snip) are free, limited per level and are not purchasable.
